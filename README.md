# PocketDrop — File Transfer

**Less emailing yourself. Save your time.**

Send photos, videos, PDFs and other files between your phone and PC with QR pairing. Choose a real folder on your PC and receive phone files directly there, without a second download step.

[Add to Chrome ↗](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ancoaipejlinofhccakcealheckdgfdm) · [Phone page](https://tasnimrimi.github.io/FileTransfer/) · [Privacy](https://tasnimrimi.github.io/FileTransfer/privacy.html)

## What PocketDrop does

- **Phone → PC:** Save incoming files directly into the PC folder you choose.
- **PC → phone:** Select files in the extension and click **To phone**. The phone starts a download; your browser may ask you to confirm it.
- **Encrypted transfers:** Files are encrypted in transit between your devices.
- **QR pairing:** Connect without creating an account or installing a separate phone app.
- **Popup convenience:** Transfers continue when the extension popup closes. Keep Chrome running and the phone page open.
- **Four themes:** Blue, green, reading and black.
- **Up to 500 MB per file:** The published version allows 524,288,000 bytes per file. Speed depends on your devices and network.

## Get started

1. Install [PocketDrop from the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ancoaipejlinofhccakcealheckdgfdm) on your PC.
2. Open PocketDrop, click **Choose PC folder**, and allow access to your chosen folder.
3. Click **Connect Phone** and scan the QR code with your phone camera.
4. Connect on the phone page and select the files you want to send.
5. Open the chosen PC folder in File Explorer to find the received files.

To send in the other direction, choose files in the PC extension and click **To phone**. If the phone browser blocks the download, use **Download didn’t start? Save again**.

Use the same home Wi-Fi with internet access for the most reliable connection. Keep both devices awake during transfers. Interrupted transfers in this version must be sent again.

## Where your files go

Phone uploads save as normal files in your selected PC folder. PocketDrop does not retain another library copy of those incoming files. Chrome may temporarily stage files while writing them.

Files added on the PC for sending use the extension’s encrypted local library. Its queued copy is removed after the phone confirms receipt; the original PC file is untouched. The phone keeps a local recovery copy until deleted because its webpage cannot confirm that a download finished. Saved files are not encrypted at rest by PocketDrop.

## Privacy and connection

No account, tracking or cloud file storage is required. GitHub Pages serves this phone webpage; it does not store your transferred files. PeerJS and Google STUN assist with connection setup and can receive network metadata. Some VPNs, guest networks and firewalls may prevent a direct connection.

Read the [privacy and connection details](https://tasnimrimi.github.io/FileTransfer/privacy.html).
